Security first

We can't read your messages. By design.

Your chats are encrypted on your phone before they leave it. Our servers only ever hold scrambled data they have no key for, and the keys never leave your device.

  • Per-device keys protected by the Android Keystore
  • Every shared file is encrypted with its own random key
  • Notifications carry no message text, only a nudge to wake the app
  • Optional app lock and passphrase-protected key backup

Features

Features

Everything WeTalks can do today, explained plainly.

Messaging

End-to-end encryption

X25519 key agreement and AES-256-GCM. Group chats use a shared group key that changes when someone is removed.

Replies, reactions, pins

Swipe a message to reply, react with animated emoji, pin important messages, forward, edit and delete for everyone.

Translate & transcribe

Translate any message into your language and read a transcript of voice notes. Hindi, Hinglish and English work out of the box.

Markdown formatting

Bold, italic, strikethrough, spoilers, lists, quotes, tables and code blocks with copy, save and HTML preview. A format bar in the message box makes it one tap.

Read aloud, naturally

Tap Listen on a message, caption or transcript and it is read in the right language, with pause and play controls in the app and the notification. Use your phone's voice, or download our optional human-like voices (31 languages including English, Hindi and Hinglish, male and female) that run entirely on your phone.

Notifications you control

Reply, mark as read or mute right from the notification. Calls ring even when the app is in the background.

Privacy controls

Choose who sees your last seen, online status and phone number. Turn read receipts off. Block anyone.

Calls

Voice and video

Peer-to-peer calls over WebRTC with echo cancellation, speaker and mute controls.

Built for video

Rounded mini window you can drag, swap and hide, camera flip, and a one-tap switch from voice to video.

Slide to answer

A smooth slide-up to answer and slide-down to decline. Nothing happens until you let go.

Files & media

Documents Coming soon

PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, CSV, e-books and archives up to 30 MB with WeTalks Plus. Dangerous file types such as apps and scripts are blocked.

Photos & camera

Take a photo or pick several, edit with crop, filters, text and shapes, add a caption and send.

Checked twice

Files are validated for type, size and content on your phone and again on our server, then re-checked when they are opened.

Better voice notes

Hold to record with live noise removal. Slide to cancel, lock for hands-free. Extra clean-up tools are coming to WeTalks Plus.

Video & audio files Coming soon

Videos show a preview in the chat and play with a built-in player. Music and audio files too. Part of WeTalks Plus.

Smart storage

See what uses space, clear the cache and control what downloads over mobile data or Wi-Fi.

Groups & channels

Groups

Add people, make admins, set who can send what and turn on slow mode. Removing someone rotates the group key.

Channels

Broadcast to followers with public or private channels and a short link such as wetalks.fireclashpro.com/c/yourchannel.

Links and QR

Share a profile or channel by link or QR code. Links open straight in the app.

Personalise

Wallpapers & bubbles

More than ten animated wallpapers and bubble styles, plus light and dark themes.

Your language

The app speaks English, Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Marathi, Telugu, Spanish, French, Russian and more.

App lock

Protect WeTalks with your fingerprint or screen lock.

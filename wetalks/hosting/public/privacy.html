Privacy policy

What WeTalks collects, why, and the choices you have. Last updated: November 2026.

WeTalks is a messaging app and service. This policy explains what information we handle. We keep it short and specific.

Information we handle

  • Account: your sign-in method (email address or phone number), display name, username, optional bio and profile photo.
  • Messages and files: stored only in encrypted form. We cannot read them. They are kept until you or the sender delete them.
  • Delivery data: which chats exist, message times, read state, online status and last seen (subject to your privacy settings), and notification tokens so we can wake your phone.
  • Developer portal: if you use it, your email, your app names and API key fingerprints (never the keys themselves) and usage counts.
  • Service messages: messages from apps that use our API are stored in your service inbox until you delete them.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data and we show no ads.
  • We do not read your end-to-end encrypted content and cannot give it to anyone.
  • We do not upload your contacts unless you choose to find friends by phone number, and then only a hash is used.

Services that help us run WeTalks

  • Google Firebase for sign-in, the message database and notifications.
  • Hostinger for this website, encrypted file storage and the developer API.
  • Online translation and speech engines, only when you ask to translate a message or transcribe a voice note. That single piece of text or audio is sent to the engine.
  • Natural voices are an optional download from our server. After that, reading aloud happens on your phone and the text is not sent anywhere.

Your choices

  • Control last seen, online status, phone number visibility and read receipts in Privacy & security.
  • Turn off or block service messages at any time.
  • Delete messages for yourself or for everyone, and clear a chat's history.
  • Ask us to delete your account and associated data.

Security

See the security page for how we protect data and the honest limits of that protection.

Children

WeTalks is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where it matters, tell you in the app.

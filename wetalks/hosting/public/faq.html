FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about WeTalks.

Is WeTalks free?
Yes. Messaging, calls, groups, channels and photo sharing are free. Some advanced voice tools will be part of an optional WeTalks Plus later.
Can you read my messages?
No. Messages and files are encrypted on your phone and we don't have the keys. See Security for exactly what we can and cannot see.
Why isn't WeTalks on Google Play?
WeTalks is in early release, so it is shared as a direct download for now. The APK is signed with a fixed key, so updates install over it normally.
I got new phone. How do I keep my old chats?
Create a key backup (Settings → Privacy & security → Back up encryption key) protected by a passphrase. On the new phone sign in and choose Restore. Without a backup, old messages can't be decrypted on a new device.
Why do I see "Can't decrypt this message"?
The message was encrypted for a key that is no longer on this phone, usually after clearing app data or reinstalling without a backup. New messages will work normally.
What files can I send?
Today you can send photos (gallery or camera), voice notes and GIFs. Video, audio files and documents up to 30 MB are coming soon with WeTalks Plus. Apps, scripts and other risky file types will always be blocked.
Do calls work when the app is closed?
Calls and messages arrive while WeTalks is running in the background. If your phone stops background apps aggressively, allow WeTalks to run in the background and enable Autostart. Instant wake-up for fully closed apps needs push services that are being rolled out.
How do I read a message aloud?
Long-press a message and tap Listen. For voice notes, tap Transcribe first. The app picks the language automatically and uses the best voice installed on your phone.
What are service messages?
Websites and apps that use the WeTalks developer API can send you one-time codes or alerts. They arrive in a separate "Service messages" inbox, never mixed with chats. You can turn them off or block any app in Privacy & security.
Is there an iPhone version?
Not yet. WeTalks is Android only for now.
How do I delete my account?
You can delete chats from inside the app at any time. To remove your account and its data completely, use the contact details on this site and we will do it.
